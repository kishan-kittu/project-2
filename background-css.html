Lord Krishna is a principal deity in Hinduism, revered as the eighth avatar of Vishnu and also as the Supreme God in his own right Wikipedia. He is often depicted as a youthful figure, playing a flute and surrounded by adoring devotees, particularly the milkmaids (Gopis). Krishna is associated with love, compassion, tenderness, and protection. He is a central figure in the Bhagavad Gita, where he imparts profound philosophical and spiritual teachings to Arjuna.
